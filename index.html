睡不好，不只是累而已Poor Sleep Is More Than Tiredness

睡眠是清理大腦、保護記憶的關鍵時間Sleep is when the brain cleans itself and protects memory

睡眠時，大腦在「大掃除」During Sleep, the Brain Cleans Up

1

清除廢物Clear Waste

睡眠時腦內的「類淋巴系統」會清走累積的毒性蛋白。The glymphatic system flushes out toxic proteins during sleep.

2

鞏固記憶Consolidate Memory

深度睡眠把白天學到的東西存進長期記憶。Deep sleep files the day's learning into long-term memory.

3

睡不好＝加速退化Poor Sleep Speeds Decline

長期睡不好，會加速失智與巴金森的病程。Long-term poor sleep can accelerate dementia and Parkinson's.

5x

阿茲海默症病人出現呼吸中止的機率，是常人的 5 倍。Sleep apnea is 5× more common in Alzheimer's disease.

常見Common

失智與巴金森病人幾乎人人都有某種睡眠困擾，卻常被忽略、沒被治療。Nearly everyone with dementia or Parkinson's has some sleep problem, yet it is often missed and untreated.

睡夢中揮拳踢腿，是重要警訊Acting Out Dreams Is a Warning Sign

REM 睡眠行為障礙（RBD）REM Sleep Behavior Disorder (RBD)

❓ 什麼是「做夢演出」？❓ What is dream enactment?

人做夢時身體本應「癱軟不動」。RBD 病人失去這層保護，把夢裡的動作真的做出來——揮拳、踢腿、大喊，甚至摔下床。Normally the body stays still while dreaming. In RBD, that protection is lost — people physically act out dreams, risking falls and injury.

⚠️ 為什麼是警訊？⚠️ Why is it a warning?

特發性 RBD 追蹤 14 年後，超過九成會發展成帕金森氏症或相關神經退化疾病，常比動作症狀早好幾年出現。Over 90% of idiopathic RBD develops Parkinson's or a related disorder within 14 years — often years before motor symptoms appear.

>90%

特發性 RBD 追蹤 14 年，發展為 α-突觸核蛋白病的比例（Continuum 2026）。Risk of progression to α-synucleinopathy over 14 years (Continuum 2026).

可以怎麼做？What to Do

① 先保護安全：床邊淨空、加護欄或地墊。① Safety first: clear bedside, add rails or padding.

② 請神經科醫師評估，及早追蹤與處置。② See a neurologist for evaluation and follow-up.

③ 有安全疑慮時，可用褪黑激素等藥物治療。③ When safety is a concern, melatonin & other medicines help.

出處：國際 RBD 症狀治療共識（Sleep Med 2025, PMID 40408791）；Continuum 2026（PMID 42544975）。Source: RBD treatment consensus (Sleep Med 2025, PMID 40408791); Continuum 2026 (PMID 42544975).

先試非藥物：睡眠衛生與光照Start Non-Drug: Sleep Hygiene & Light

不花錢、最安全，永遠是第一步Free, safest, always the first step

1

固定起床時間Fixed Wake Time

每天同一時間起床，比早睡更重要。Same rise time daily — more important than bedtime.

2

白天多照光Bright Light by Day

早上曬太陽或坐在窗邊，校準生理時鐘。Morning sunlight resets the body clock.

3

午睡別太久Short Naps Only

午睡控制在 30 分鐘內，下午 3 點前。Keep naps under 30 min, before 3 pm.

4

睡前放鬆Wind Down

睡前少用 3C、少喝咖啡茶，保持昏暗安靜。Less screen and caffeine; dim, quiet bedroom.

為什麼光照有用？Why Light Works

光是「校準生理時鐘」最強的訊號。規律的白天照光，能讓晚上自然想睡，改善日夜顛倒與失眠。Light is the strongest signal that sets the body clock, helping night sleep and easing day-night reversal.

研究：光照治療可減少夜間醒來；需規律、固定時間、足夠照度才有效。Studies: light therapy reduces nocturnal awakenings; it needs consistent timing and intensity.

出處：光照治療系統性回顧（Sleep Med Rev 2020, PMID 32289734）；認知障礙睡眠介入統合分析（IJERPH 2025, PMID 40566383）。Source: Sleep Med Rev 2020 (PMID 32289734); IJERPH 2025 (PMID 40566383).

非藥物：運動、呼吸中止、認知行為治療Non-Drug: Exercise, Apnea, CBT-I

找出背後原因，對症處理更有效Find the underlying cause and treat it directly

🏃

白天運動Daytime Exercise

散步、伸展、太極都好。研究顯示日間運動能改善夜間睡眠品質，對巴金森病人尤其有幫助。Walking, stretching, tai chi all help. Daytime exercise improves night sleep, especially in Parkinson's.

😮‍💨

打鼾？查呼吸中止Snoring? Check for Apnea

大聲打鼾、白天嗜睡，可能是呼吸中止。戴呼吸器（CPAP）能顯著改善睡眠與白天精神。Loud snoring plus daytime sleepiness may be sleep apnea. CPAP markedly improves sleep and alertness.

🧠

失眠認知行為治療CBT for Insomnia

CBT-I 教我們「床只跟睡覺連結」，是治療失眠最有效、又沒有副作用的方法。CBT-I trains the brain to link bed with sleep — the most effective, side-effect-free insomnia treatment.

💡 重點：睡不好常常有「背後的原因」——疼痛、夜尿、憂鬱、藥物、咖啡因。先找出並處理原因，比急著吃安眠藥更根本。💡 Key point: poor sleep often has an underlying cause — pain, night urination, depression, medication, caffeine. Find and treat the cause before reaching for a sleeping pill.

出處：認知障礙睡眠介入統合分析（IJERPH 2025, PMID 40566383）；Neurotherapeutics 2021（PMID 33179197）。Source: IJERPH 2025 (PMID 40566383); Neurotherapeutics 2021 (PMID 33179197).

居家安全，與「何時該就醫」Home Safety & When to See a Doctor

先保護安全，再談治療Protect safety first, then treat

1

床邊安全Bedside Safety

做夢演出或夜間起身，床邊淨空、加護欄或地墊防摔。For dream enactment or night wandering: clear the bedside, add rails or padding.

2

夜間防跌Prevent Falls

夜燈、防滑、廁所通道暢通，藥物引起的頭暈要跟醫師說。Night light, non-slip floors, clear path to toilet; report any medication dizziness.

3

照護者也要睡Caregivers Need Sleep Too

照護者長期陪夜容易垮掉，要輪班、找喘息資源。Caregivers on night duty burn out — take shifts and seek respite support.

出現這些，請就醫See a Doctor If…

🚩 睡夢中揮拳踢腿、傷到自己或同床者🚩 Acting out dreams, hurting self or bed partner

🚩 白天嗜睡到影響開車、走路會跌倒🚩 Daytime sleepiness affecting driving or walking

🚩 大聲打鼾、半夜呼吸停頓🚩 Loud snoring with breathing pauses

🚩 日夜顛倒嚴重，全家都撐不下去🚩 Severe day-night reversal overwhelming the family

出處：RBD 共識（Sleep Med 2025, PMID 40408791）；巴金森睡眠共識（Parkinsonism Relat Disord 2026, PMID 42731925）。Source: Sleep Med 2025 (PMID 40408791); Parkinsonism Relat Disord 2026 (PMID 42731925).

失眠用藥：哪些好、哪些要避開Insomnia Medicines: Which Help, Which to Avoid

先用溫和的，避開會傷腦、易跌倒的藥Choose gentle options; avoid those harming the brain or causing falls

✅ 相對安全、優先考慮✅ Safer, Preferred

褪黑激素：溫和、副作用少；低劑量開始（巴金森研究顯示較高劑量更有效）。Melatonin: gentle, few side effects; start low (higher doses more effective in Parkinson's).

食慾素拮抗劑（新藥）：如 suvorexant、lemborexant，改善睡眠且對認知較友善，需注意跌倒。Orexin antagonists (newer): e.g. suvorexant, lemborexant — improve sleep and are more cognition-friendly; watch for falls.

⚠️ 盡量避開⚠️ Avoid

安眠藥（BZD／Z-drug）：如 zolpidem，增加跌倒與骨折、記憶與認知風險。Sleeping pills (BZD / Z-drugs): e.g. zolpidem — raise fall, fracture and memory risk.

抗組織胺（感冒藥常見）：長期用增加認知退化與譫妄。Antihistamines (common in cold medicines): long-term use raises cognitive decline and delirium.

抗精神病藥：除非行為症狀必要，否則有中風、死亡風險，不當安眠藥用。Antipsychotics: stroke and mortality risk — never use just for sleep unless behavior symptoms require it.

出處：失智睡眠治療系統性回顧（J Geriatr Psychiatry Neurol 2026, PMID 41871321）；Neurotherapeutics 2021（PMID 33179197）；褪黑激素於巴金森（Sleep Med 2025, PMID 40288252）。Source: J Geriatr Psychiatry Neurol 2026 (PMID 41871321); Neurotherapeutics 2021 (PMID 33179197); Sleep Med 2025 (PMID 40288252).

「做夢演出」的用藥Medicines for Dream Enactment

RBD 有安全疑慮時，兩種藥是首選When RBD threatens safety, two medicines are first-line

1

褪黑激素Melatonin

國際共識推薦的 RBD 首選之一，安全性高、副作用少，睡前服用。A consensus-recommended first choice for RBD — high safety, few side effects, taken at bedtime.

2

氯硝西泮（clonazepam）Clonazepam

也是首選，效果明確，但低劑量開始；研究顯示長期使用對認知的影響不大，仍需注意跌倒與呼吸中止。Also first-line and effective, started at low dose. Long-term use shows little effect on cognition, but watch for falls and sleep apnea.

💡 國際睡眠醫學會目前推薦 RBD 用藥共四種：氯硝西泮、褪黑激素、rivastigmine、pramipexole。用哪一種、劑量多少，一定要由神經科醫師依個別狀況決定，不可自行購買或停藥。💡 The American Academy of Sleep Medicine currently recommends four agents for RBD: clonazepam, melatonin, rivastigmine, and pramipexole. Which one and what dose must be decided by a neurologist — never self-medicate or stop on your own.

出處：國際 RBD 症狀治療共識（Sleep Med 2025, PMID 40408791）；clonazepam 認知研究（J Clin Sleep Med 2024, PMID 38494993）。Source: Sleep Med 2025 (PMID 40408791); J Clin Sleep Med 2024 (PMID 38494993).

不寧腿與白天嗜睡的處理Restless Legs & Daytime Sleepiness

常被忽略，卻能明顯改善生活品質Often missed, yet highly treatable

不寧腿（腿痠癢麻、想動）Restless Legs Syndrome

① 先檢查是否缺鐵，缺鐵要補鐵。① Check iron levels; supplement if low.

② 藥物用 α2δ 類（如 gabapentin、pregabalin），相對安全。② α2δ-ligands (gabapentin, pregabalin) are relatively safe.

③ 巴金森病人併不寧腿常見，卻高達九成沒被發現。③ RLS is common in Parkinson's, yet over 90% goes undiagnosed.

白天嗜睡Daytime Sleepiness

① 先看巴金森用藥，某些多巴胺藥會引起嗜睡，請醫師調整。① Review Parkinson's drugs first — some dopamine agents cause sleepiness; ask your doctor to adjust.

② 白天小量咖啡因可能有幫助。② A little daytime caffeine may help.

③ 嗜睡嚴重會影響開車，甚至有生命危險，務必就醫。③ Severe sleepiness makes driving dangerous — see a doctor.

出處：PD 睡眠藥理綜述（Expert Opin Pharmacother 2024, PMID 39535843）；PD 併不寧腿（Sleep Med 2026, PMID 42721723）；PD 白天嗜睡（Sleep Med Clin 2025, PMID 40912747）。Source: Expert Opin Pharmacother 2024 (PMID 39535843); Sleep Med 2026 (PMID 42721723); Sleep Med Clin 2025 (PMID 40912747).

用藥注意事項，與最新研究Medication Cautions & New Research

藥不是吃了就沒事，這些要盯著Medicine needs monitoring

1

防跌倒Prevent Falls

多數助眠藥都可能頭暈、無力，起夜要慢、要扶。Most sleep medicines can cause dizziness; rise slowly, hold on.

2

看交互作用Check Interactions

同時吃巴金森、心臟、感冒藥，一定要讓醫師知道全部用藥。Tell the doctor all medicines you take — Parkinson's, heart, cold medicines.

3

定期回診Regular Follow-up

睡眠藥要定期評估效果與副作用，不要一直吃、也不要自己停。Review sleep medicines regularly for effect and side effects; don't stop on your own.

🆕 藥物新知：食慾素拮抗劑🆕 New Frontier: Orexin Antagonists

新一代失眠藥（suvorexant、lemborexant 等）作用在「清醒開關」，不是強行壓制大腦。研究顯示能改善失智病人的睡眠，對認知較友善，甚至有初步證據可能有助清除腦內毒性蛋白。目前仍在積極研究中。Newer insomnia drugs (suvorexant, lemborexant) target the "wake switch" instead of forcing the brain down. They improve sleep in dementia, are more cognition-friendly, and early evidence hints they may aid brain protein clearance. Research is ongoing.

出處：DORA 於 AD 綜述（Am J Geriatr Psychiatry 2025, PMID 39462720；J Alzheimers Dis 2026, PMID 42312432）；suvorexant 譫妄試驗（JAMA Netw Open 2024, PMID 39150711）。Source: Am J Geriatr Psychiatry 2025 (PMID 39462720); J Alzheimers Dis 2026 (PMID 42312432); JAMA Netw Open 2024 (PMID 39150711).
